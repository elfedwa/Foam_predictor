        Science Background

        Foam: gas bubbles separated by thin liquid films.

        Stable clue: many small bubbles stay separated over time.

        Failure clue: drainage, coalescence, coarsening, wall breakage, or collapse patches.

        Model judge: believable predictions change step by step, not by sudden jumps.
